# methodical-ep.com

The home page of Methodical Enterprises LLC, served by GitHub Pages from this repository at
<https://methodical-ep.com> (and <https://wheatbit.github.io>).

- `index.html` is the whole page: the company, its apps and links to their privacy policies.
  It loads nothing from other sites (no fonts, scripts or trackers).
- The privacy policies live in [WheatBit/AppPrivacy](https://github.com/WheatBit/AppPrivacy) and
  appear under `/AppPrivacy/` on the same domain.
- `logo.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `favicon.png` are the company logo at 256, 180 and 64 px.

Nothing sensitive belongs here: this repository is public.
